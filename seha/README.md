# Rafiqa (رفيقة) — Women's Health Companion

**An open-source, Arabic-first digital health companion for rural women and girls**, developed by **SADEEM for Innovation and Sustainability Solutions LLC** (Belbeis, Sharqia, Egypt).

🌐 **Live prototype:** https://ahmed15500.github.io/sadeem/seha/

## The problem
Rural women and girls in Egypt's Sharqia Governorate face distance, cost, stigma, low literacy and low bandwidth barriers to trustworthy maternal and reproductive health information and care. Frontline public services (health units and family medicine centres) exist, but women often do not know what services are available, when to seek care, or which symptoms are emergencies.

## What Rafiqa does (prototype v0.1)
- Plain-language Arabic health education on pregnancy, antenatal care, nutrition, family planning, menstrual health, breastfeeding, postnatal care and mental wellbeing.
- **Voice read-aloud for every section** (Web Speech API) — designed for low-literacy users.
- A prominent **danger-signs panel** with clear "go now" guidance and the national ambulance number (123).
- **"Ask Rafiqa"** — an on-device Arabic FAQ assistant (keyword-based today; an AI/ML layer with medically reviewed content is the funded next step).
- **Privacy by design:** the prototype collects no personal data and runs entirely on the user's device.

## Roadmap with funding
1. Medically reviewed, expanded Arabic content library (with Egyptian public-health reviewers), including audio-first navigation.
2. AI assistant layer (retrieval over reviewed content only) with safeguarding, consent architecture and escalation to human services.
3. Community-health-worker / facilitator mode for SADEEM's existing rural women's groups in Sharqia villages.
4. Pilot with women's groups across SADEEM's community network (300+ rural households, 4 community points), measurement & evaluation, and a full open-source release with documentation for replication.

## Built on a field-tested platform
SADEEM already operates a live community platform (EcoWasteAI) serving 300+ subscribing rural households through 4 collection points in Sharqia, 3 days a week — the community trust, facilitator network and digital infrastructure Rafiqa builds on. Rafiqa was developed and first tested in Egypt.

## Disclaimer
Rafiqa provides general health education only and is not a diagnostic or treatment tool. In an emergency call 123 (Egypt ambulance) or go to the nearest hospital.
